Reading Passage & Grammar Context

In modern education, mastering English is not just about memorizing isolated rules—it is about recognizing context signals and communicating ideas clearly. While younger learners build habits through daily routines (every day, usually, at the moment), high school students analyze how clauses connect, how passive and causative structures shift focus (have something done), and how academic vocabulary conveys precise meaning.
